## Do the math on the "noncitizen voter" panic

Here's the part that should end the conversation. Texas ran its entire voter roll, more than 18 million people, through the federal citizenship check last year. It flagged 2,724 "potential noncitizens" ([Texas Secretary of State](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/about/newsreleases/2025/102025.shtml)). <span class="m">D</span> Then 578 of them proved they were citizens, and the state referred 117 cases for investigation ([Texas Secretary of State](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/about/newsreleases/2026/091526.shtml)). <span class="m">D</span>

Late Tuesday, with votes still being counted, Gina Hinojosa led Gov. Abbott by about 583,000 votes ([MultiState, partial returns](https://www.multistate.us/elections/governor-race/texas-2026)). <span class="m">R</span>

That's more than 200 times every name Texas flagged, before anyone checked whether they're citizens. It's almost twice Secretary Mullin's own claim that noncitizens on voter rolls "top 300,000" *nationwide*. Toss every one of them out of Texas alone and the result doesn't change. <span class="m">I</span>

Not every race is a blowout. We're still watching Alaska and Missouri, and close House seats will take days. But this is why Plan A was always the SAVE America Act and stopping ballots from being cast in the first place: after the fact, the numbers aren't there. And by the skin of our teeth, the checks held. Sen. Thune wouldn't blow up the filibuster for it, and when it came up anyway, four Republicans joined Democrats to vote it down ([Democracy Docket](https://www.democracydocket.com/news-alerts/senate-rejects-bid-to-revive-save-america-act-but-the-war-isnt-over/)). The courts stopped the ballot holds. No voter confusion, no intimidation wave, no reason to send anyone to the polls. And even with the whole framework in place, what's the point of fighting certification if you still wouldn't have the numbers?

## Why we're not stopping

Because it isn't over just because they lost. Mullin, on Fox, on Election Day:

> "After this election we'll be gathering all the data, we'll be gathering the voting logs" ... "Even past this election, it is still DHS' job to make sure" that "only those that were legally able to vote voted." ([Democracy Docket](https://www.democracydocket.com/news-alerts/mullin-says-trump-admin-will-comb-voting-logs-to-hunt-for-noncitizens-after-election/)) <span class="m">R</span>

If the next two years go to using this machine to track down and deport people who have every right to be here, that's not a knee-jerk reaction. That's the long-term plan. A government that already has the data shouldn't need new contract add-ons or a large-scale "fraud" operation to check a voter roll. If they buy them anyway, we'll be here with the receipts. And we'll be popping popcorn for Secretary Mullin's post-election "debrief."
