INSPECTORS GENERAL · THE HUB’S PARTNERS
The matching exemption
A data match run by an inspector general, or by any agency “in coordination with” one, during an audit or investigation “shall not be considered a matching program” (IG Act §6(j), added 2016). No matching agreement, no Federal Register notice, no independent check or notice to the person before adverse action. IG data requests are also exempt from the Paperwork Reduction Act (§6(k)). D

READING THE MAP

Follow the red. The inputs the court record describes (state rolls, DOJ’s 50.6M, the 48.2M “independent” records, commercial data) enter through a DHS intake (the court record names no office) and USCIS’s fraud unit and become TECS records. The California list was built by USCIS FDNS outside SAVE, using Accurint plus direct SSA and State queries (Bartell Decl. #2), refined by HSI with unnamed law-enforcement systems, and delivered to DOJ and LA County on Oct. 13. PCIS was planned to query TECS for display (PIA 2022); whether that went live is not shown. PCIS, USCIS’s one-profile-per-person index, sits on the same data layer as a lake with an entity-resolution graph built “for investigators.” No court order names PCIS or the data lake. Whether voter data is stored there is not shown. The PCIS privacy assessment calls its index a “single, trusted source.”
