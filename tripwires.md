# Tripwire board

Last updated: Sunday Nov 1, 2026, ~7:00 pm ET. Updated as news breaks.
Status: 🔴 FIRED (it happened) · 🟠 ARMED (the pieces are in place or it is pending) · ⚪ NOT YET (nothing seen).
Marks: D document · R reporting · I our inference.

## Since this morning
- **Alaska: Judge Gleason dismissed DOJ's suit Nov 1.** The court "lacks the authority to order that certain ballots cast by Alaskans be segregated so that federal law enforcement officials can conduct an unspecified investigation of the voters who cast them." DOJ appealed the same day (D).
- **DOJ asked to pause Judge Thompson's order** protecting California election officials (D, Nov 1). That order only bars demands made "solely on the basis" of a federal ineligibility claim, which leaves warrants and individual cases open.
- **Washington: the federal list admits it can include citizens.** The U.S. Attorneys' Oct 26 letter says the people listed "are or were noncitizens (i.e., they may have subsequently naturalized)," asks officials not to verify or count those ballots, and says a second DHS list is coming before Election Day (D).
- **Partner states:** DOJ paid Mississippi's attorney general more than $2.5 million in July to build a joint federal-state fraud task force, "a first-of-its-kind model" (D). Ohio's secretary of state says his office "uses federal law enforcement resources and data" on the voter roll and referred a registration group to the FBI (Oct 26, D). Georgia's said an AI agent reading public election data "is not a hack" (D).
- **DOJ's poll monitors:** in its own court filing, DOJ says monitors won't act as law enforcement "as commonly understood"; prosecutors and investigators will serve, and DEA, ATF and Bureau of Prisons staff aren't ruled out (D).
- **ICE is re-buying its investigative-data subscription** (the Accurint/CLEAR layer used to "enrich" voter matches), with feeds into "Palantir platform, PenLink, and ICE Data Analytics" (D, Oct 22).
- **New on the [phrase trail](../phrase-trail/): "What they promised, and what they left room for,"** the exact words of federal assurances about agents, troops and ballots, and what each leaves open.

## The sequence we're watching
Foreign-hack claim → federal "who is a real voter" list → ballots held → cyber incident blamed abroad → FBI takes the "investigation" and the evidence → list used to set aside ballots or block certification before Jan 3.
