# Tripwire board

Last updated: Sat Oct 31, 2026, ~10:30 am ET. Updated as news breaks.
Status: 🔴 FIRED (it happened) · 🟠 ARMED (the pieces are in place or it is pending) · ⚪ NOT YET (nothing seen).
Marks: D document · R reporting · I our inference.

## Since Thursday
- **DOJ put every state's ballots under a federal criminal warning (letters dated Oct 29).** Election officials in all 50 states were told to preserve pollbooks, envelopes and the ballots themselves for 22 months: "It is a crime to willfully fail to comply" (R). DOJ never answered the senators who asked it to promise not to seize ballots (tripwire 14 is now FIRED).
- **The Ninth Circuit said no, 2-1, to holding ~20,000 California ballots** (Oct 30). DOJ is reported to be heading to the Supreme Court (R).
- **Alaska: DOJ asked a court to "preserve and segregate all ballots ranking Daniel J. Sullivan,"** and the U.S. Attorney announced a "significant surge of law enforcement assets to Alaska" (Oct 30, R).
- **DOJ and DHS told Judge Boasberg they pressed five states:** California and Washington (DOJ), plus DHS's July letters to Nevada, Pennsylvania and New Jersey. Phone calls and meetings were left out (R). He rules Mon Nov 2.
- **Checks that held:** Judge Thompson barred DOJ from threatening election workers (expires after Nov 3), and the Ninth Circuit kept the nine-state ban on DHS election field operations (R).

## The sequence we're watching
Foreign-hack claim → federal "who is a real voter" list → ballots held → cyber incident blamed abroad → FBI takes the "investigation" and the evidence → list used to set aside ballots or block certification before Jan 3.
