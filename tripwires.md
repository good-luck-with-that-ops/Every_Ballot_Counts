## What we're watching for
The board tracks several ways federal power could reach this election. They overlap, and none needs the others to happen.
- **Scaring voters away:** armed agents near polls, door-knocks, enforcement surges, threats of prosecution. Tripwires 7, 8, 15.
- **Challenging voters and their ballots:** federal "noncitizen" lists, demands to remove voters or set ballots aside, and partner states purging or sidelining voters with unverified data. Tripwires 4, 5, 16.
- **Pressuring the people who count:** criminal threats, grand-jury subpoenas and preservation demands aimed at election officials. Tripwires 2, 6, 14.
- **Targeting candidates and blocking winners:** citizenship and fraud probes of candidates, suits to stop certification, and challenges to seating members when the House convenes Jan 3. Tripwires 9, 10.
- **Investigating organizers:** domestic-terrorism and fraud investigations of registration, union, observer and protest groups (NSPM-7). See "Also watching."
- **The worst case, a declared emergency:** foreign-hack claim → federal "who is a real voter" list → ballots held → cyber incident blamed abroad → FBI takes the "investigation" and the evidence → list used to set aside ballots or block certification before Jan 3. The machine claim (China-made parts, "compromised" systems) and a "significant incident" declaration are the two hinges. Tripwires 1, 3, 13, 17.
- **What stays after Jan 3:** new data powers and weakened oversight that carry into 2028. Tripwires 11, 12.

## Checked every day by name (the hinges of the worst case)
- **"Significant incident" (row 3):** the exact phrase, 6 U.S.C. 677 and 677b, CISA emergency directives under 44 U.S.C. 3553(h) (and whether one names an election-related system or the EAC), any "major incident" notice to Congress (due within 7 days of one) by DOJ, DHS, SSA or the EAC, and, before any notice, any claim that federally held voter data was "accessed," "exfiltrated," "compromised" or "breached" (100,000+ people makes it a major incident automatically), the Federal Register (a declaration must be published within 72 hours), and the Cyber Response and Recovery Fund.
- **The voting-machine claim (row 17):** China-made parts, "foreign adversary" components, "compromised" or "unusable" machines, EO 13873, Commerce, CISA advisories naming a vendor, hand-count demands, the DHS post-election "debrief."
- **Every statement by DHS Secretary Mullin:** DHS releases, his Truth Social and X posts, TV and radio interviews, hearings and letters, searched by name each day, not only when another story mentions him.
