## Since Sunday
- **A federal judge blocked DOJ from demanding that California election officials set aside ballots or change their procedures based on the federal "noncitizen" lists** (D, CHIRLA v. DHS, D.D.C., Chief Judge Boasberg, Nov 2). The first ruling on the merits: the Executive Branch "has likely violated the Constitution's Elections Clause," which leaves election rules to states and Congress, and DOJ's aiding-and-abetting threat against officials is "a red herring." The court found CHIRLA's expert credible: at least 22 of its citizen members are on the list for certain, 66 more nearly certain.
- **The Supreme Court turned down DOJ's emergency request to make California set aside about 20,000 ballots** (R, NPR/KPBS, CNN, WaPo, Nov 2 evening), hours after DOJ filed it. That makes four courts in a row (Carter, the Ninth Circuit, Alaska's federal court and now the Supreme Court) refusing to hold ballots before the count.
- **DOJ asked a judge to consider contempt, and a criminal-contempt referral to DOJ itself, against the immigrant-rights group whose experts tested the federal "noncitizen" list** (R, PBS/AP Nov 1). The test had found likely citizens on the list and ~42% of names missing from California's voter file. No ruling yet.
- **The copy of the list DOJ handed over was cut down:** 8 fields instead of about 30, no California voter ID number, file name ending "reduced columns" (R, Democracy Docket Nov 1).
- **DOJ told Judge Boasberg its letters change nothing "absent the intervention of a federal court,"** and that more than 30 states refused its voter-data demands (R, Nov 1 filing). *Sequence:* a court order is the step DOJ is still seeking, in the Alaska appeal and in California (I). Boasberg's ruling is still pending.
- **DHS's general counsel, on TV Oct 31: "we will find you, we will charge you, and we will deport you"**; says 200+ charged in noncitizen-voting cases (R).
- **The same HSI tools are being used on political organizers (R, WIRED Oct 28; Unicorn Riot Nov 1; D, defense filing):** in the Minnesota 15 case, about 119 HSI agents and 11 undercovers investigated anti-ICE organizers under operations defense lawyers tie to NSPM-7, using Thomson Reuters CLEAR, Treasury financial-intelligence queries (including SEIU-related records), PenLink and commercial data brokers, the same tools in the voter-data pipeline. Defense lawyers call it "a nationwide effort to investigate opponents of ICE and DHS that began with the issuance of NSPM-7" (argument, not a finding).
- **The White House is planning to govern without Congress if Democrats win (R, Reuters; Telegraph via Daily Beast):** Miller, Vought and the counsel's office have worked since April on executive actions on immigration, tariffs, housing and spending, and "where he could push the limits of his authority."
- **Checked and not seen as of Monday morning:** a second DHS list to Washington, any warrant for ballots or equipment, an emergency or "significant incident" declaration, or any U.S. official blaming a current hack on a foreign country.

## What we're watching for
The board tracks several ways federal power could reach this election. They overlap, and none needs the others to happen.
- **Scaring voters away:** armed agents near polls, door-knocks, enforcement surges, threats of prosecution. Tripwires 7, 8, 15.
- **Challenging voters and their ballots:** federal "noncitizen" lists, demands to remove voters or set ballots aside. Tripwires 4, 5.
- **Pressuring the people who count:** criminal threats, grand-jury subpoenas and preservation demands aimed at election officials. Tripwires 2, 6, 14.
- **Targeting candidates and blocking winners:** citizenship and fraud probes of candidates, suits to stop certification, and challenges to seating members when the House convenes Jan 3. Tripwires 9, 10.
- **Investigating organizers:** domestic-terrorism and fraud investigations of registration, union, observer and protest groups (NSPM-7). See "Also watching."
- **The worst case, a declared emergency:** foreign-hack claim → federal "who is a real voter" list → ballots held → cyber incident blamed abroad → FBI takes the "investigation" and the evidence → list used to set aside ballots or block certification before Jan 3. Tripwires 1, 3, 13.
- **What stays after Jan 3:** new data powers and weakened oversight that carry into 2028. Tripwires 11, 12.
