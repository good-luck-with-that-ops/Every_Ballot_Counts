Independent, citizen research on the federal systems, contracts and data flows that touch election data ahead of the **Nov 3, 2026 midterms**. Everything here is built from public records: Federal Register notices, privacy assessments, procurement and USAspending records, court filings, hearings and news reports.

**This is not an investigation with inside sources, and it does not allege wrongdoing by any vendor or person.** Every claim carries an evidence mark so you can weigh it: **D** primary document · **R** reporting · **I** inference · **U** unresolved · **K** general knowledge, verify.

## Start here

- **[Architecture map](map/architecture.html)**: one big picture of the systems, who holds which records, and 57 numbered data flows. Best on a large screen; there is also a [PNG](assets/architecture.png).
- **[System Registry](registry/)**: one entry per system, contract vehicle or data feed: what it holds, how we know, what is still unresolved.
- **[Phrase Trail](phrase-trail/)**: where the same wording recurs across statutes, directives, procurement documents and reports.
- **[About and method](about.html)**: how this is built, how to correct it, how to reuse it.
- **[One-page overview (PDF)](Every_Ballot_Counts_Overview.pdf)** and the [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@EveryBallotCounts).
- **[Downloads (Google Drive)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1flNo25-HOs3o105u02uywLL_iw84vElx)**: PDF copies, if GitHub is unfamiliar.
- **[Updates](updates.html)**: what changed and when.

## How to use it

Reporters and researchers: each registry entry lists the records to pull next under "Open questions/records (U)", and the registry's appendix lists priority records to request. Corrections with a source are welcome.
