# About and method

**Who:** an independent individual researching this in a personal capacity. It is not the position of any employer or organization.

**What this is:** a public, living reference that connects public documents about federal election-data systems. It started Oct 26, 2026 and is updated as records are found.

**What this is not:** not legal advice, not an official source, and not an accusation. Vendors are treated as neutral infrastructure. A company is named only where a public document ties it to a system, and nothing here implies a company knows of or takes part in any use described.

## Method

1. Start from capability and demonstrated willingness: what a system can technically do, and what has been publicly shown.
2. Mark every claim: D primary document, R reporting, I inference, U unresolved, K general knowledge to verify.
3. Keep competing explanations open. A recurring phrase or a shared vendor is a lead, not a conclusion.
4. Check each new document against the registry and log the change.
